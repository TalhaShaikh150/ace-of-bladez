Situated in the heart of Pewsey Vale, Ace Of Bladez is a traditional men's barbers with a modern edge.

Designed around a concept of traditional Italian barbering & style we offer you our expertise in both classic and contemporary cutting techniques so you can ace your game.

We treat every customer as a unique individual and as such tailor every cut specifically designed to meet your needs. Whether it is a classic wet shave or a modern edgy fade with bespoke design, our barbers have it covered.

We take pride in delivering a memorable experience so that you leave our shop feeling fantastic, looking fantastic and smelling fantastic... because you are fantastic.
